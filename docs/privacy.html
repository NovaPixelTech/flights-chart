Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no personal profiles, no ads.

What this app stores in your browser

Search settings, automations and saved runs live in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device unless you export them. There is no account and no server-side profile.

Anonymous analytics

We use Umami, a cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics service. It records aggregate information:

  • page views and approximate visitor counts;
  • country, referrer, browser, operating system and device type;
  • anonymous product events — a search was started or completed, a comparison partner link was clicked, or a search failed (error category only).

We do not collect the cities, airports or dates you type, and we do not store cookies, raw IP addresses or cross-site identifiers. Analytics data is never sold or used for advertising.

Opting out

Analytics is optional. You can disable it in your browser at any time by running this in the developer console:

localStorage.setItem('umami.disabled', '1')

Clearing your browser storage re-enables it. An ad-blocker will also prevent the analytics script from loading.

Third-party services

Searches are fetched through the reader service configured in the app, and clicking a result opens Google Flights, Kiwi.com, Skyscanner or Gotogate. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
